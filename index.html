A calm, gentle story every night

Nodland is a bedtime-story app for kids, built around one simple idea: a calm, gentle story every night, told with your child's name in it. Everything works fully offline. No account, no ads, and no internet needed — open the app at bedtime and it just works.

What's inside

📚

365 stories

120 Short, 125 Medium, and 120 Long pre-written stories — one for every day of the year, plus 10 bonus Thanksgiving stories.

⭐

Made yours

Add your child's name as the hero, one character (a family member, a friend, or a pet), and your town — and the story is told with them in it.

🌙

Tonight's Story

Can't choose? Tonight's Story picks one for tonight, so bedtime starts without a debate.

🎵

Lullabies & rain

5 gentle music-box lullabies plus calming rain sounds, with a sleep timer that fades out.

🌜

One-tap wind-down

Tonight's story, followed by the lullaby or rain sound you picked — one tap and the evening settles.

🎙️

Your own voice

Record stories in your own voice. Recordings stay on your device only — they are never uploaded or shared.

♥

Favorites & pick-up

Save favorites and pick up where you left off, right at the sentence you paused on.

⏰

Bedtime reminder

A gentle daily reminder when bedtime approaches, plus a sleep timer for stories.

📴

Fully offline

No account, no ads, no internet. Non-stimulating before bedtime: no videos, no stimulating images.
